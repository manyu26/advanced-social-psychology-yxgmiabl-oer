# 1.1 What social psychology studies

<!-- slide -->
## Three things about a person
{{mermaid
graph LR
  A["Affect<br/>what they feel"] --> D["shaped by<br/>social presence<br/>and situations"]
  B["Behavior<br/>what they do"] --> D
  C["Cognition<br/>what they think"] --> D
  D --> E["To explain<br/>and to predict"]

Not merely describing what happens — saying why, and what would happen under conditions not yet observed.

<!-- slide main-side -->
## The move in Allport's 1954 definition
<!-- @main -->
The field is defined by the presence of other human beings — and that presence counts as

==**actual, imagined, or implied**==

- Alone in a room, believing you might be observed → in scope
- Shaped by a group you will never meet → in scope
<!-- @side -->
::: info
Social psychology is not the study of interaction.

It is the study of **what other minds do to a mind** — including when they are not there.

*Read the original sentence: Allport, 1954, p. 5.*
:::

<!-- slide -->
## What kind of creature is a person?
Social psychology has never settled on one model. Baumeister's inventory:

:::: cols
::: col
- **Hot** processes (motivation) / **cold** processes (cognition)
- **Information seeker** — explains events
- **Consistency seeker** — resolves dissonance
- **Situational responder**
- **Group member**
- **Self-esteem maximizer**

# 1.2 A field defined by its arguments

<!-- slide 2col -->
## Two ways to write a history
<!-- @left -->
{{sp[red] **Progress narrative**}}
- A sequence of discoveries
- Each improves on the last
- The present looks like an endpoint
- New findings have nowhere to attach
<!-- @right -->
{{sp[blue] **Tension narrative**}}
- Opposing positions, both serious
- Both generated real research
- Neither side conceded
- Any paper can be *located* in it

<!-- slide -->
## What makes a "tension"
{{sp[info] Definition}} **Tension** — opposing positions on an important issue that attracted proponents *and* adversaries, and galvanized sustained empirical and theoretical attention.

1. The field has taken **different positions from its inception to the present**
2. The **ebb and flow** of the debate is itself instructive

::: warning
Two pitfalls named: **presentism** (today's position narrated as the resolution) and **American centrism** (one tradition mistaken for the field).
:::

# 1.3 A short history, and the rise of the experiment

<!-- slide -->
## Older than the discipline
:::: cols
::: col
**Religious and philosophical roots**
- Judeo-Christian scriptures — free will, prosocial and antisocial conduct, self, motives, attribution, forgiveness
- Bhagavad Gita — motivation, self, social influence
- Rule of Benedict (6th c.) — individual responsibility, interdependence
- Codex Hammurabi (~1760 BCE) — reciprocity as law
:::
::: col
**The Greeks**
- **Aristotle** — humans inherently social; must understand the social environment
- **Plato** — group function, social contract, obedience, conformity, social facilitation, social loafing
- **Socrates vs the Sophists** — arguably the first two-route model of persuasion
:::
::::

<!-- slide -->
## Five periods
| Period | The turn |
|---|---|
| **1850–1930** Emergence | Cattaneo coins *psicologia sociale* (1864) · Wundt's *Völkerpsychologie* · **Triplett's first experiment, 1897** · Floyd Allport's individualist turn |
| **1930–1945** Maturation & migration | Attitudes research · frustration–aggression · **Lewin flees to the US**, founds the RCGD at MIT |
| **1946–1969** Flourishing | Golden age of the lab experiment · Asch, Milgram, Festinger, Deutsch, Kelley · Division 8 (1947) · **JPSP (1965)** |
| **1970–1990** Ascent of social cognition | Gergen's and McGuire's critiques · Heider, Kelley, Nisbett & Ross, Kahneman & Tversky · dual-process models |
| **1990–present** Biology, culture, big data | fMRI · beyond WEIRD · shift to self-report · **reproducibility crisis** → Module 2 |

<!-- slide -->
## What the timeline shows that a list of findings would not
::: danger
- The first experiment ran in **1897**. The method did not take over until the **1950s**.
- The individual/collective split was a **decision** taken in the 1920s, not a discovery.
- The critiques that opened the 1970s — culture-bound findings, a demographically narrow field, one-variable designs — are ==recognizably the critiques of the 2010s==.
:::
**Tensions, not progress.**

<!-- slide -->
## It was not obvious
::: warning
**Wundt** endorsed the experiment for cognition and perception — and explicitly **excluded** social psychological principles from its remit.
:::
- No other social science used experiments at the time
- Triplett's 1897 study sat essentially idle for **fifty years**
- So experimental social psychology had to be **made possible**, not simply adopted

<!-- slide main-side -->
## Push 1 — Floyd Allport (1924)
<!-- @main -->
Restricted social behavior to **individual behavior directed toward social objects**.

- Large sociocultural forces → ==hard to experiment on==
- Small, isolable elements → ==clean designs become feasible==
- Definition and method were settled **together**

Compare **Ross (1908)**: the field is about uniformities arising from the *interplay of minds* — far closer to sociology.
<!-- @side -->
::: danger
**The casualty:** culture — the phenomenon that would have kept particularism visible.
:::

<!-- slide -->
## Push 2 — Kurt Lewin
{{mermaid
graph TD
  L["Lewin's metatheory"] --> A["Person and environment"]
  L --> B["Genotypic process beneath<br/>phenotypic observation"]
  L --> C["Situations as<br/>systems under tension"]
  L --> D["RCGD at MIT — trained Festinger,<br/>Schachter, Deutsch, Thibaut, Kelley"]
}}
Against Allport, for whom any group psychology reduced without remainder to individual psychology.

<!-- slide main-side -->
## Push 3 — Leon Festinger
<!-- @main -->
- Took over Lewin's graduate cohort after Lewin's death in 1947
- Favored **mini-theories** — social comparison, cognitive dissonance
- Defined theory as **causal relations between variables** → elevates the experiment
<!-- @side -->
::: info
Late-1970s SESP survey, three greatest contributors:

**79%** Festinger
**70%** Lewin
No one else above 50%
:::

<!-- slide 2col -->
## What is an experiment *for*?
<!-- @left -->
{{sp[blue] **Lewin**}}
- Capture the **genotypic structure** of a phenomenon
- Transposable to other situations
- Confounds tolerable if structure is revealed
- Aspired to physics-like universal theory
- {{sp[red] **Failed**}}
<!-- @right -->
{{sp[green] **Festinger**}}
- Manipulate **single variables** under control
- **Empirical** purity over theoretical purity
- Meticulous tests of conceptual relations
- Aspired to rigor like non-social psychology
- {{sp[success] **Succeeded**}}

<!-- slide step -->
## Example — was the experiment simply "how science works"?
**Q.** A colleague says the field adopted the experiment because that is just how science is done. What four facts must they explain away?

1. **Wundt excluded social psychology** from the experimental remit
2. **The first experiment sat idle for fifty years** — 1897 to the 1950s
3. **The subject matter had to be redefined first** — Allport's restriction made clean designs conceivable
4. **Two committed experimentalists wanted incompatible things** — Lewin and Festinger disagreed

::: success
**Takeaway:** the experiment's dominance was *achieved*, not discovered — which is not to say it was wrong.
:::

<!-- slide template=section -->
# 1.4 Tension 1 — Particularism vs universalism

<!-- slide 2col -->
## One question, two scopes
<!-- @left -->
**Broad form**
Are some social psychological processes **permanent, unvarying equipment** of our species?

- Largely dormant in the early decades
- Still unsettled
<!-- @right -->
**Narrow form**
What can an experiment reveal **beyond its own operationalizations**?

- {{sp[info] External validity}}
- Dominated the field's attention instead

<!-- slide -->
## Four distinct critiques — not four versions of one
| Critique | The charge |
|---|---|
| **Artificiality** | Lab situations are artificial and unrepresentative |
| **Humanity and agency** | Experiments disregard the participant as an agent |
| **Paradigm narrowness** | Too few paradigms, often not themselves validated |
| **Anti-positivism** | Universal truths may not exist to be found |

::: warning
A rebuttal that answers one leaves the other three standing.
:::

<!-- slide -->
## Where this surfaced historically
- **Gergen (1973)** — social psychology cannot be a science on the model of physics, because social behavior varies with **historical time and culture**
- Same period: the field's theorists are **demographically narrow** — the ancestor of the WEIRD critique
- **McGuire (1973)** — designs are simplistic, one variable at a time
- Culture taken seriously only from the **1990s**

::: danger
Fifty years old. Module 2's material is its direct descendant — not a recent emergency.
:::

<!-- slide template=section -->
# 1.5 Tension 2 — Social context vs the individual

<!-- slide main-side -->
## The road not taken
<!-- @main -->
The group-level alternative was **available from the start and set aside, not refuted**:

- **Cattaneo (1864)** — the psychology of *associated* minds
- **Wundt** — mental products of a community, inexplicable through individual consciousness
- **Ross (1908)** — what arises from the interplay of minds

All predate Allport's 1924 restriction.
<!-- @side -->
::: info
**The European chorus**

**Tajfel** — intergroup behavior as a meta-theory

**Moscovici** — minority influence; social representations
:::

<!-- slide -->
## Groupy in theory, individualist in practice
Lewin's RCGD trained the next generation, and group dynamics flourished — Festinger on informal communication, Deutsch on cooperation, Asch on conformity, Kelley on interdependence, later the risky shift, groupthink, deindividuation, jury deliberation.

::: warning
Yet most of it remained **individualist in its unit of explanation** — what a person does when a group is present. Basic group research has since declined even as applied group research flourished.
:::
==That pattern is the theory/practice gap in one sentence.==

# 1.6 Tension 3 — Rationality vs irrationality

<!-- slide -->
## The move that reframed the debate
{{mermaid

  B --> C["Separate outcomes<br/>from processes"]
  C --> D["Biased outcome,<br/>rational process"]

## The evidence this tension runs on
<!-- @left -->
**The social-cognition programme**
- **Heider** — common-sense psychology
- **Kelley** — covariation: consensus, distinctiveness, consistency
- **Nisbett & Ross** — inference, and the principle of **construal**
- **Kahneman & Tversky** — heuristics
<!-- @right -->
**Perception failing in replicable ways**
- Misattribution of arousal — the **suspension bridge**
- The **weapon effect**
- **Mere exposure** — even for unrecognizable stimuli: briefly-shown Chinese characters, and tones played to chicks *before hatching*

<!-- slide step -->
## Example — is the bias real?
**Q.** A paradigm built specifically to elicit a bias reliably elicits it. What follows?

1. **Not** that the bias is fake — a designed probe can still detect something real
2. **Not** that the bias is general — the paradigm arranged the conditions
3. What follows is a **burden of proof**: show it appears where nobody arranged for it
4. Note this is the ==paradigm-narrowness critique== from 1.4, arriving from another direction

::: success
**Three newer arguments:** bias vs accuracy · bias as artifact of design · survival, not accuracy, is the criterion
:::

# 1.7 Tension 4 — Dispassionate scientist vs value-driven advocate

<!-- slide -->
## Three debates people keep conflating
1. How researchers' **values shape the questions** asked and answers found
2. Whether **advocacy is faithful** to the strength of the science
3. Whether psychologists should **advocate at all**

::: warning
These have different answers. An argument that settles one settles none of the others.
:::

<!-- slide 2col -->
## Can science be value-free?
<!-- @left -->
**Weber**
- Social science is necessarily **value-relevant**
- All knowledge of cultural reality is from a point of view
- But it *should* be free of value **judgment**
<!-- @right -->
**McMullin**
- The standards for judging a theory are themselves value-laden
- Bohr weighted **predictive accuracy**
- Einstein weighted **consistency** with existing knowledge
- Choosing between them requires values

<!-- slide main-side -->
## Where it bites
<!-- @main -->
**Reicher and Haslam** — the Stanford Prison Experiment and Milgram's obedience paradigm contributed to the creation of **ethics review boards**.

Those boards weigh **societal benefit** against **participant harm** — a value judgment that determines which research is permissible, and therefore which discoveries can be made.
<!-- @side -->
::: danger
**Follow the consequence.**

Ethics constraints are part of why the field moved from behavioral measures toward **self-report** from the 1990s.

A value judgment reshaped the field's **evidence base**.
:::

<!-- slide -->
## Two worked cases
:::: cols
::: col
**Lewin's action research**
Wartime work using group pressure to shift household food choices. The field's most influential methodologist was also its most committed applied researcher.
:::
::: col
**Desegregation and the jigsaw classroom**
Simultaneously a scientific programme, a policy instrument, and a moral commitment — so all three value debates apply at once.
:::
::::

# 1.8 The other lens — themes and pillars

<!-- slide -->
## Why there is a second lens
Miller and Laurin chose tensions **because** the previous edition's history — Ross, Lepper and Ward (2010) — had already taken up the field's enduring themes and foundational insights.

::: info
Two Handbook editions organize the same material completely differently. That is itself the best evidence for the claim that **a history is made by its lens**.
:::

<!-- slide -->
## Three central themes
{{mermaid
graph TD
  A["Norms &amp; situational influence<br/>what people do in groups<br/>that they never do alone"] --> D["Social psychology's<br/>accumulated insight"]
  B["Subjectivity &amp; construal<br/>situations are interpreted,<br/>never objective"] --> D
  C["Non-obviousness<br/>findings contrary<br/>to expectation"] --> D
}}
Norms → groupthink, deindividuation, social facilitation, later culture and stereotype threat. Construal → the prisoner's dilemma changes with framing. Non-obviousness → drinking norms, proximity and attraction, the smoke-filled room, foot-in-the-door.

<!-- slide -->
## Four pillars
| Pillar | What it names |
|---|---|
| **Naïve realism** | We see the world objectively; those who disagree must be uninformed, irrational or biased |
| **Lay dispositionism** | Over-attributing to stable traits — the fundamental attribution error, false confession, eyewitness error |
| **Overconfidence** | Needs to seem consistent, rational and moral; self-serving bias, downward comparison, positive illusion |
| **Confirmation bias** | Including self-fulfilling prophecy — expectations producing their own confirmation |

<!-- slide -->
## Why experience does not correct lay dispositionism
{{mermaid
graph LR
  A["People choose<br/>their situations"] --> B["Situations then guide and<br/>constrain their behavior"]
  B --> C["Person and situation are<br/>confounded in everything observed"]
  C --> D["The trait explanation<br/>always seems to fit"]
}}
::: warning
Even social psychologists underestimate situational power — and a **self-serving asymmetry** lets us see the situation's grip on ourselves but not on others.
:::

## Two lenses, one field
<!-- @left -->
{{sp[blue] **Tensions**}}
The field as **unresolved argument**.

Positions have serious proponents on both sides; the movement of a debate is evidence; any paper can be located.

*Good for reading critically.*
<!-- @right -->
{{sp[green] **Themes and pillars**}}
The field as **accumulated insight**.

Things social psychology has learned about people and largely agrees on.

*Good for knowing what the discipline contributes.*

::: danger
**Not rivals.** The pillars *are* positions inside the tensions — lay dispositionism is the person/situation question turned into an object of study; naïve realism and confirmation bias are findings about rationality.
:::

      // sync without re-triggering the change handler (already handled below).
      suppressChange = true;
      cm.setValue(preamble);
      suppressChange = false;
    }
    if (cm) cm.setOption('theme', cmTheme());
    markDirty();
    if (API.reveal) setTimeout(function () { API.reveal.layout(); API.refresh(); }, 60);
  }

  // ---- dirty / save (self-reproducing) -------------------------------------
  function markDirty() { if (!dirty) { dirty = true; root.setAttribute('data-dirty', '1'); hostPostDirty(true); } }
  function clearDirty() { if (dirty) hostPostDirty(false); dirty = false; root.setAttribute('data-dirty', '0'); }

  // ---- host embedding (orz-host-save@1) -------------------------------------
  // When a platform embeds this file in an iframe and announces the
  // orz-host-save protocol (spec: PROTOCOL.md in the orz-mdhtml repo), Save
  // posts the document to the host instead of touching the file system. Never
  // enabled without the host's hello; protocol messages are accepted only from
  // window.parent, and after the handshake only from the recorded host origin.
  // Message content is read as data, never evaluated. Export keeps working.
  var HOST_PROTOCOL = 'orz-host-save';
  var HOST_VERSION = 1;
  var hostOrigin = null;    // recorded at handshake; null = unhosted
  var hostSaveTimer = null; // watchdog for a save awaiting acknowledgement

  function isHosted() { return hostOrigin !== null; }
  // An opaque embedder (sandboxed/srcdoc host) serializes as the string 'null',
  // which postMessage rejects as a targetOrigin — fall back to '*' (the payload
  // contains nothing the host doesn't already have).
  function hostTarget() { return hostOrigin && hostOrigin !== 'null' ? hostOrigin : '*'; }
  function hostPost(msg) { try { window.parent.postMessage(msg, hostTarget()); } catch (e) {} }
  function hostPostDirty(d) {
    if (!isHosted()) return;
    hostPost({ type: 'orz-host-dirty', protocol: HOST_PROTOCOL, version: HOST_VERSION, dirty: !!d });
  }
  function hostSave(src, html) {
    if (hostSaveTimer) return; // one save in flight at a time
    hostSaveTimer = setTimeout(function () {
      hostSaveTimer = null;
      toast('Save failed — no response from the host'); // document intact, still dirty
    }, 10000);
    hostPost({ type: 'orz-host-save', protocol: HOST_PROTOCOL, version: HOST_VERSION, source: src, html: html, theme: currentTheme });
  }

  // ---- host AI assistant (orz-host-ai@1) ------------------------------------
  // When the host advertises AI operations, selecting text in the editor shows
  // an "Improve selection" affordance; picking an op sends the passage to the
  // host, which returns a suggested replacement to apply. File owns the UI; the
  // host owns the model + governance. Additive — no host, no affordance.
  var AI_PROTOCOL = 'orz-host-ai';
  var AI_VERSION = 1;
  var aiOps = null;      // advertised operations, or null when no AI host
  var aiOrigin = null;   // recorded at the AI handshake
  var aiSeq = 0;
  var aiPending = {};    // requestId -> resolve
  var aiTrigger = null;  // the floating "Improve selection" chip
  var aiPanel = null;    // the menu / result popover
  var aiRect = null;     // anchor rect {left,top,bottom} for the active popover

  function aiTarget() { return aiOrigin && aiOrigin !== 'null' ? aiOrigin : '*'; }
  function aiPost(msg) { try { window.parent.postMessage(msg, aiTarget()); } catch (e) {} }
  function aiRequest(op, text, sel) {
    return new Promise(function (resolve) {
      var id = 'ai' + (++aiSeq);
      aiPending[id] = resolve;
      aiPost({ type: 'orz-host-ai-request', protocol: AI_PROTOCOL, version: AI_VERSION, requestId: id, op: op, text: text, selection: !!sel });
      setTimeout(function () { if (aiPending[id]) { delete aiPending[id]; resolve({ ok: false, error: 'No response from the host.' }); } }, 30000);
    });
  }

  function aiBox() {
    var b = document.createElement('div');
    b.style.cssText = 'position:fixed;z-index:80;background:Canvas;color:CanvasText;border:1px solid GrayText;border-radius:10px;box-shadow:0 6px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.28);font:13px system-ui,sans-serif;padding:4px;';
    return b;
  }
  // Position `el` (already in the DOM, so it can be measured) just below the
  // selection end; if it would overflow the bottom, flip it ABOVE the selection;
  // if it fits neither, pin near the top and let it scroll. Clamp horizontally.
  function aiSelRect() {
    var to = cm.cursorCoords(cm.getCursor('to'), 'window');
    var fr = cm.cursorCoords(cm.getCursor('from'), 'window');
    return { left: to.left, top: fr.top, bottom: to.bottom };
  }
  function aiElRect(el) { var b = el.getBoundingClientRect(); return { left: b.left, top: b.top, bottom: b.bottom }; }
  function aiPlace(el) {
    if (!el) return;
    var r = aiRect || (cm ? aiSelRect() : { left: 8, top: 8, bottom: 40 });
    var w = el.offsetWidth || 240;
    var h = el.offsetHeight || 40;
    var left = Math.max(8, Math.min(r.left, window.innerWidth - w - 8));
    var top = r.bottom + 6;
    if (top + h > window.innerHeight - 8) {
      var above = r.top - h - 6;
      if (above >= 8) {
        top = above;
      } else {
        top = 8;
        el.style.maxHeight = (window.innerHeight - 16) + 'px';
        el.style.overflowY = 'auto';
      }
    }
    el.style.left = left + 'px';
    el.style.top = top + 'px';
  }
  function aiHidePanel() { if (aiPanel) { try { aiPanel.remove(); } catch (e) {} aiPanel = null; } }
  function aiHideTrigger() { if (aiTrigger) { try { aiTrigger.remove(); } catch (e) {} aiTrigger = null; } }
  function aiHideAll() { aiHidePanel(); aiHideTrigger(); }

  }

  function aiRefresh() {

  // ---- host-provided includes (orz-host-include@1) --------------------------
  // When a host offers include resolution, `{{md-include URL}}` / `{{markdown
  // URL}}` directives are resolved by the host (which owns the fetch + allowlist)
  // and inlined into the PREVIEW render only. The deck source keeps the
  // directive. STANDALONE (no host) the directives are left as-is — the file
  // NEVER auto-fetches a URL. (See PROTOCOL.md.)
  var INCLUDE_PROTOCOL = 'orz-host-include';
  var INCLUDE_VERSION = 1;
  var includeOrigin = null, includeSeq = 0, includePending = {}, includeCache = {}, includeInflight = {};
  function includeTarget() { return includeOrigin && includeOrigin !== 'null' ? includeOrigin : '*'; }
  function includePost(msg) { try { window.parent.postMessage(msg, includeTarget()); } catch (e) {} }
  function includeRequest(url) {
    return new Promise(function (resolve) {
      var id = 'inc' + (++includeSeq);
      includePending[id] = resolve;
      includePost({ type: 'orz-host-include-request', protocol: INCLUDE_PROTOCOL, version: INCLUDE_VERSION, requestId: id, url: url });
      setTimeout(function () { if (includePending[id]) { delete includePending[id]; resolve(null); } }, 30000);
    });
  }
  function applyIncludes(src) {
    if (!includeOrigin) return src;
    return src.replace(/\{\{(?:markdown|md-include)\s+(https?:\/\/[^\s}]+)\}\}/g, function (whole, url) {
      var v = includeCache[url]; return (typeof v === 'string') ? v : whole;
    });
  }
  function ensureIncludes(src) {
    if (!includeOrigin) return;
    var re = /\{\{(?:markdown|md-include)\s+(https?:\/\/[^\s}]+)\}\}/g, m, seen = {};
    while ((m = re.exec(src))) {
      var url = m[1];
      if (seen[url]) continue; seen[url] = true;
      if (Object.prototype.hasOwnProperty.call(includeCache, url) || includeInflight[url]) continue;
      includeInflight[url] = true;
      (function (u) { includeRequest(u).then(function (md) { includeCache[u] = (typeof md === 'string') ? md : null; delete includeInflight[u]; scheduleIncludeRerender(); }); })(url);
    }
  }
  // The source used for RENDERING (web includes inlined); save always uses the
  // raw `fullSource()`, so the deck keeps its `{{md-include}}` directives.
  function renderSource() { var s = fullSource(); ensureIncludes(s); return applyIncludes(s); }

  // Re-render EVERY slide's content in place when an include resolves — update
  // each <section>'s innerHTML without rebuilding the section list, so reveal.js
  // keeps its slide count + current position (a full renderAll would reset them).
  function rerenderAllInPlace() {
    var deck = API.parseDeck(renderSource());
    var secs = sections();
    if (deck.slides.length !== secs.length) { API.renderAll(renderSource()); return; }
    for (var i = 0; i < secs.length; i++) {
      var tmp = document.createElement('div');
      tmp.innerHTML = API.renderSlide(deck.slides[i], API.md, deck.config);
      var fresh = tmp.firstElementChild;
      if (fresh) secs[i].innerHTML = fresh.innerHTML;
    }
    try { API.reveal.sync(); } catch (e) {}
    API.refresh();
  }
  var includeRerenderTimer = null;
  function scheduleIncludeRerender() {
    if (includeRerenderTimer) clearTimeout(includeRerenderTimer);
    includeRerenderTimer = setTimeout(rerenderAllInPlace, 80);
  }

  function onHostMessage(event) {
    // only the embedding parent may speak the protocol
    if (window.parent === window || event.source !== window.parent) return;
    var d = event.data;
    if (!d || typeof d !== 'object') return;
    // after the handshake, hold the parent to the origin it introduced itself with
    if (isHosted() && hostOrigin !== 'null' && event.origin !== hostOrigin) return;
    if (d.type === 'orz-host-hello' && d.protocol === HOST_PROTOCOL && typeof d.version === 'number' && d.version >= 1) {
      hostOrigin = event.origin;
      // reply with the highest version we support ≤ the host's (we speak only 1)
      hostPost({ type: 'orz-host-ready', protocol: HOST_PROTOCOL, version: HOST_VERSION, kind: 'slides' });
      if (dirty) hostPostDirty(true); // catch the host up on edits made pre-handshake
    } else if (d.type === 'orz-host-saved' && hostSaveTimer) {
      clearTimeout(hostSaveTimer); hostSaveTimer = null;
      if (d.ok) { clearDirty(); toast('Saved'); }
      else { toast('Save failed' + (d.error ? ' — ' + String(d.error) : '')); }
    } else if (d.type === 'orz-host-ai-hello' && d.protocol === AI_PROTOCOL && Array.isArray(d.operations)) {
      aiOrigin = event.origin;
      aiOps = d.operations.filter(function (o) { return o && o.id && o.title; });
      aiPost({ type: 'orz-host-ai-ready', protocol: AI_PROTOCOL, version: AI_VERSION });
      // Reveal the toolbar's page-wide AI button — runs an op on the editor buffer.
      var aiBtn = document.getElementById('orz-ai');
      if (aiBtn && aiOps.length) {
        aiBtn.style.display = '';
        aiBtn.onclick = function () { if (cm) aiOpen(aiElRect(aiBtn), cm.getValue(), false); };
      }
    } else if (d.type === 'orz-host-ai-result' && d.requestId && aiPending[d.requestId]) {
      var aiRes = aiPending[d.requestId]; delete aiPending[d.requestId];
      aiRes({ ok: !!d.ok, proposed: d.proposed, error: d.error });
    } else if (d.type === 'orz-host-include-hello' && d.protocol === INCLUDE_PROTOCOL && typeof d.version === 'number' && d.version >= 1) {
      includeOrigin = event.origin;
      includePost({ type: 'orz-host-include-ready', protocol: INCLUDE_PROTOCOL, version: INCLUDE_VERSION, kind: 'slides' });
      scheduleIncludeRerender(); // request + render now that web includes can resolve
    } else if (d.type === 'orz-host-include-result' && d.requestId && includePending[d.requestId]) {
      var incRes = includePending[d.requestId]; delete includePending[d.requestId];
      incRes(d.ok ? d.markdown : null);
    }
  }
  // listen from script load so an early hello isn't missed
  window.addEventListener('message', onHostMessage);

  function serializeDoc() {
    var clone = root.cloneNode(true);
    var deckEl = clone.querySelector('#orz-deck');
    if (deckEl) deckEl.textContent = '\n' + escapeSource(fullSource()) + '\n';
    clone.setAttribute('data-mode', 'present');
    clone.setAttribute('data-theme', currentTheme);
    clone.removeAttribute('data-dirty');
    // never bake in the (edit-only) update banner so a viewer can't see it
    var ub = clone.querySelector('#orz-update'); if (ub) { ub.classList.remove('show'); ub.removeAttribute('data-latest'); }
    // Reset reveal's rendered DOM so the reopened file re-renders from #orz-deck.
    var reveal = clone.querySelector('.reveal');
    if (reveal) { reveal.className = 'reveal'; reveal.innerHTML = '<div class="slides"></div>'; }
    // Reset the live editor back to a clean textarea.
    var ed = clone.querySelector('#orz-editor-host');
    if (ed) ed.innerHTML = '<textarea id="orz-ta" spellcheck="false"></textarea>';
    return '<!DOCTYPE html>\n' + clone.outerHTML + '\n';
  }

  function idbOpen() {
    return new Promise(function (res, rej) {
      var r = indexedDB.open('orz-slides', 1);
      r.onupgradeneeded = function () { r.result.createObjectStore('handles'); };
      r.onsuccess = function () { res(r.result); };
      r.onerror = function () { rej(r.error); };
    });
  }
  function idbGet(key) {
    return idbOpen().then(function (db) {
      return new Promise(function (res, rej) {
        var t = db.transaction('handles', 'readonly');
        var g = t.objectStore('handles').get(key);
        g.onsuccess = function () { res(g.result || null); };
        g.onerror = function () { rej(g.error); };
      });
    }).catch(function () { return null; });
  }
  function idbPut(key, val) {
    return idbOpen().then(function (db) {
      return new Promise(function (res, rej) {
        var t = db.transaction('handles', 'readwrite');
        var p = t.objectStore('handles').put(val, key);
        p.onsuccess = function () { res(); };
        p.onerror = function () { rej(p.error); };
      });
    }).catch(function () {});
  }
  function pickAndStore() {
    return window.showSaveFilePicker({
      suggestedName: (CFG.filename || 'deck') + '.slides.html',
      types: [{ description: 'Slides HTML', accept: { 'text/html': ['.slides.html', '.html'] } }],
    }).then(function (h) { fileHandle = h; if (CFG.docId) idbPut(CFG.docId, h); return h; });
  }
  function acquireHandle() {
    if (fileHandle) return Promise.resolve(fileHandle);
    if (!CFG.docId) return pickAndStore();
    return idbGet(CFG.docId).then(function (saved) {
      if (!saved || !saved.queryPermission) return pickAndStore();
      return saved.queryPermission({ mode: 'readwrite' }).then(function (p) {
        if (p === 'granted') return saved;
        return saved.requestPermission({ mode: 'readwrite' }).then(function (p2) {
          return p2 === 'granted' ? saved : null;
        });
      }).then(function (h) { if (h) { fileHandle = h; return h; } return pickAndStore(); });
    }).catch(function () { return pickAndStore(); });
  }
  function isServed() { return location.protocol === 'http:' || location.protocol === 'https:'; }

  function save() {
    if (cm) { slides[curIndex] = cm.getValue(); writeDeck(); }
    var html = serializeDoc();
    // A hosting platform (verified handshake) receives the save instead of the
    // file system; the host acknowledges with orz-host-saved (see PROTOCOL.md).
    if (isHosted()) { hostSave(fullSource(), html); return; }
    if (isServed() && !fileHandle) { if (dirty) showServedNote(); return; }
    if (window.showSaveFilePicker) {
      acquireHandle()
        .then(function (h) { return h.createWritable(); })
        .then(function (w) { return Promise.resolve(w.write(html)).then(function () { return w.close(); }); })
        .then(function () { clearDirty(); toast('Saved'); })
        .catch(function (err) { if (err && err.name === 'AbortError') return; downloadFile(html); clearDirty(); toast('Saved a local copy'); });
    } else {
      downloadFile(html); clearDirty(); toast('Saved a local copy');
    }
  }
  function downloadFile(text) {
    var blob = new Blob([text], { type: 'text/html' });
    var url = URL.createObjectURL(blob);
    var a = document.createElement('a');
    a.href = url; a.download = (CFG.filename || 'deck') + '.slides.html';
    document.body.appendChild(a); a.click(); a.remove();
    setTimeout(function () { URL.revokeObjectURL(url); }, 1000);
  }
  function exportCopy() {
    if (cm) { slides[curIndex] = cm.getValue(); writeDeck(); }
    downloadFile(serializeDoc()); toast('Downloaded a local copy');
  }
  function showServedNote() { var n = document.getElementById('orz-served-note'); if (n) n.classList.add('show'); }

  // ---- version check -------------------------------------------------------
  function isNewer(a, b) {
    var pa = String(a).split('.'), pb = String(b).split('.');
    for (var i = 0; i < 3; i++) {
      var x = parseInt(pa[i], 10) || 0, y = parseInt(pb[i], 10) || 0;
      if (x > y) return true; if (x < y) return false;
    }
    return false;
  }
  // SECURITY: the update source is HARDCODED here, never read from the file's
  // config — a tampered/forged file cannot redirect "Update" to attacker code.
  // Host is fixed to jsDelivr/HTTPS; the exact URLs are confirmed with the user.
  // (Protects genuine files; a wholly-malicious file controls this code too — see
  // the README security note. Clicking Update trusts npm + jsDelivr.)
  var UPD = {
    host: 'https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/',
    manifest: 'https://data.jsdelivr.com/v1/packages/npm/orz-slides-browser/resolved',
    enginePkg: 'orz-slides-browser', engineFile: 'orz-slides.browser.js', appPkg: 'orz-slides'
  };
  function checkVersion() {
    if (!CFG.rendererVersion) return;
    try {
      var cached = JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem('orz-slides:vercheck') || 'null');
      if (cached && (Date.now() - cached.t) < 86400000) {
        if (cached.v && isNewer(cached.v, CFG.rendererVersion)) showUpdate(cached.v);
        return;
      }
    } catch (e) {}
    fetch(UPD.manifest).then(function (r) { return r.json(); }).then(function (j) {
      var latest = j && j.version;
      try { localStorage.setItem('orz-slides:vercheck', JSON.stringify({ t: Date.now(), v: latest })); } catch (e) {}
      if (latest && isNewer(latest, CFG.rendererVersion)) showUpdate(latest);
    }).catch(function () {});
  }
  function showUpdate(latest) {
    var bar = document.getElementById('orz-update'); if (!bar) return;
    bar.querySelector('.upd-text').textContent = 'Framework ' + latest + ' available (file uses ' + CFG.rendererVersion + ').';
    bar.setAttribute('data-latest', latest);
    bar.classList.add('show');
  }
  /** One-click update: re-fetch the engine bundle + app.js at the latest version,
   *  re-inline them, bump the version, save in place, and reload. */
  function applyUpdate() {
    var bar = document.getElementById('orz-update'); var latest = bar && bar.getAttribute('data-latest'); if (!latest) return;
    var engineUrl = UPD.host + UPD.enginePkg + '@' + latest + '/' + UPD.engineFile;
    var appUrl = UPD.host + UPD.appPkg + '@' + latest + '/assets/app.js';
    if (!window.confirm('Update the framework to ' + latest + '?\n\nThis downloads and runs code from:\n  ' + engineUrl + '\n  ' + appUrl + '\n\nOnly proceed if you trust this document and its publisher.')) return;
    toast('Downloading framework ' + latest + '…');
    Promise.all([
      fetch(engineUrl).then(function (r) { if (!r.ok) throw new Error('engine'); return r.text(); }),
      fetch(appUrl).then(function (r) { if (!r.ok) throw new Error('app'); return r.text(); }),
    ]).then(function (res) {
      var es = document.querySelector('script[data-orz-asset="engine"]');
      if (es) { if (es.getAttribute('src')) es.setAttribute('src', engineUrl); else es.textContent = res[0]; }
      var as = document.querySelector('script[data-orz-asset="app"]');
      if (as) as.textContent = res[1];
      var cs = document.querySelector('script[data-orz-asset="config"]');
      if (cs) { CFG.version = latest; CFG.rendererVersion = latest; cs.textContent = 'window.__ORZ_SLIDES__ = ' + JSON.stringify(CFG) + ';'; }
      bar.classList.remove('show');
      var html = serializeDoc();
      if (isServed() && !fileHandle) { showServedNote(); return; }
      if (window.showSaveFilePicker) {
        return acquireHandle()
          .then(function (h) { return h.createWritable(); })
          .then(function (w) { return Promise.resolve(w.write(html)).then(function () { return w.close(); }); })
          .then(function () { toast('Updated to ' + latest + ' — reloading…'); setTimeout(function () { location.reload(); }, 700); });
      }
      downloadFile(html); toast('Updated copy downloaded — reopen it to use the new framework.');
    }).catch(function () { toast('Update failed — check your connection.'); });
  }

  // ---- toast ---------------------------------------------------------------
  var toastTimer = null;

  function buildLayoutMenu() {
    var menu = document.getElementById('orz-layout-menu'); if (!menu) return;
    menu.innerHTML = LAYOUTS.map(function (l) {
      return '<button class="orz-layout-tile" type="button" role="menuitem" data-layout="' + l.key + '" title="' + l.name + '">' + l.thumb + '<span class="lname">' + l.name + '</span></button>';
    }).join('') +
      '<a class="orz-layout-more" href="https://markdown.orz.how/slides.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">More layouts &amp; syntax →</a>';

      var t = e.target.closest ? e.target.closest('.orz-layout-tile') : null;
      if (!t) return;

      closeLayoutMenu();
    });

  function openLayoutMenu() {
    var menu = document.getElementById('orz-layout-menu'), btn = document.getElementById('orz-layout-btn');
    if (!menu || !btn) return;
    menu.hidden = false;
    var r = btn.getBoundingClientRect(), w = menu.offsetWidth || 320, h = menu.offsetHeight;
    menu.style.left = Math.max(8, Math.min(r.left, window.innerWidth - w - 8)) + 'px';

    btn.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'true');
  }

    var menu = document.getElementById('orz-layout-menu'), btn = document.getElementById('orz-layout-btn');

  }
  function toggleLayoutMenu() {

  // Apply a layout to the current slide. New/empty slide → drop the skeleton in;
  // a slide with content → keep the skeleton and tuck the old content into an
  // HTML comment (with any nested "-->" neutralised) so it can be copy-pasted.
  function applyLayout(key) {
    var l = layoutByKey(key); if (!l || !cm) return;
    if (editingDeck) { toast('Switch to a slide to apply a layout'); return; }
    var src = cm.getValue();
    var m = src.match(/^[ \t]*<!--\s*slide\b[^]*?-->[ \t]*\r?\n?/);
    var body = m ? src.slice(m[0].length) : src;
    var trimmed = body.replace(/^\s+|\s+$/g, '');
    var isEmpty = trimmed === '' || /^##\s+new slide$/i.test(trimmed);
    var next;
    if (isEmpty) {
      next = l.md;
    } else {
      var safe = body.replace(/-->/g, '--​>').replace(/\s+$/, '');
      next = l.md.replace(/\s+$/, '') +
        '\n\n<!--\nPrevious content — move it into the regions above, then delete this block.\n\n' +
        safe + '\n-->\n';
    }
    cm.setValue(next);
    cm.focus();
    markDirty();
  }

  // ---- boot ----------------------------------------------------------------
  function boot() {
    if (!API) return; // engine bundle missing
    currentTheme = root.getAttribute('data-theme') || CFG.defaultTheme;
    var sel = document.getElementById('orz-theme');
    if (sel) {
      var opts = (CFG.themes || []).map(function (t) { return '<option value="' + t.id + '">' + t.name + '</option>'; }).join('');
      sel.innerHTML = opts; sel.value = currentTheme;
    }
    // The deck's <!-- deck theme: --> may differ from the file's data-theme; if a
    // saved override link exists it already wins. Keep currentTheme in sync.
    if (root.getAttribute('data-theme') && root.getAttribute('data-theme') !== currentTheme) currentTheme = root.getAttribute('data-theme');
    applyInlineTheme(currentTheme); // assert the active inline theme on load

    function start() {
      loadParts();
      writeDeck();
      updatePos();
      positionEditCtrl();
      if (API.reveal && API.reveal.on) {
        API.reveal.on('slidechanged', function () {
          curIndex = curH();
          if (editing && !editingDeck) loadSlideIntoEditor(curIndex);
          updatePos();
          positionEditCtrl();
        });
      }
      window.addEventListener('resize', function () { setTimeout(positionEditCtrl, 80); });
      [300, 900].forEach(function (t) { setTimeout(positionEditCtrl, t); });
    }
    // The engine mounts on DOMContentLoaded too; wait until reveal exists.
    if (API.reveal) start();
    else {
      var tries = 0;
      var iv = setInterval(function () {
        if (API.reveal || tries++ > 100) { clearInterval(iv); start(); }
      }, 30);
    }
    wireUi();
    // version check runs on entering edit (edit view only), not on load
  }

  if (document.readyState === 'loading') document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', boot);
  else boot();
})();
